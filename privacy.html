Privacy Policy

Greg Local Time (GLT) · Last updated: November 11, 2026

The short version

GLT works out the time on your device, using your location. Your location leaves your device only if you choose to share it, and then only as a rounded, anonymous point on a public map. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Who runs this

GLT is made by Greg Chaprnka, an individual. Contact: greglocaltime@gmail.com.

What stays on your device

The iPhone app, Apple Watch app, widgets, and complications use your location to calculate sunset. That calculation happens on your device. Your settings and last known location are stored on your device and are not sent anywhere by the watch app, widgets, or complications.

The public map (optional, off by default)

If you opt in, the iPhone app sends one record, at most once an hour, containing:

  • your latitude and longitude rounded to 0.1° (about 7 miles / 11 km), and
  • the date and time.

We don't send your name, email, device ID, or advertising ID. Records aren't linked to you. Manually entered coordinates are never sent. You can turn this off at any time in the app's Settings.

Records are stored in Google Firebase and shown on a public map at greglocaltime.com/map.html. Anyone can see them. Because records are anonymous, individual ones can't be identified or deleted on request.

The website

If you allow your browser to share your location, the site sends your coordinates to BigDataCloud to look up a place name, and adds a point to the public map (coordinates rounded to 0.1°, place name, date and time). The map page also loads code from cdnjs and map tiles from OpenStreetMap, which can see your IP address as with any website.

Third parties

Google (Firebase), BigDataCloud, OpenStreetMap, cdnjs, Ko-fi, and Apple. Each has its own privacy policy. We don't use ad networks or third-party analytics.

Tips (optional)

GLT is free, and nothing in it is locked behind a payment. On the website, an optional link leads to my tip page on Ko-fi (ko-fi.com/greglocaltime). Tipping unlocks nothing. Ko-fi handles the payment, so I don't receive your payment details, and its own privacy policy applies once you open the link. The website doesn't share your location or other data with Ko-fi. Tips are not tax-deductible and are not donations to a charity. The iPhone app doesn't offer tips.

Children

GLT is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect their personal information.

Your choices

Decline or revoke location access in your device Settings, decline or turn off map sharing in the app, or enter coordinates manually.
